How to read this chart Structure / sentiment / alignment / pressure

Structure strip

The thin strip under price summarizes setup quality. Green is stronger structure, red is weaker structure, and mixed zones show transition.

Sentiment MA 21

The low-key gold path is the emotional trend scaled onto the price pane so direction can be compared without adding another panel.

Alignment

Hover the Sentiment MA 21 path to compare scaled sentiment versus price. Sentiment premium means sentiment is above the price path; price premium means price is ahead.

Decision Pressure

The hover also shows pressure, skew, source, and gate. Treat it as setup tension and confirmation context, not a standalone trade signal.

SETA reads attention, participation, and structure. It is designed to explain market emotion and setup quality, not to predict exact prices or issue trade instructions.
