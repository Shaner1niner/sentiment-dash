# SETA Public Chart Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary explains the public dashboard chart layer in subscriber-friendly language.

It is reader education, not model logic.

## Chart elements

### Structure strip

The thin strip under the price pane summarizes setup quality.

- green means stronger structure
- red means weaker structure
- mixed zones show transition or uncertainty

### Sentiment MA 21

The gold Sentiment MA 21 path is the emotional trend.

It is scaled into the price pane so users can compare direction and position against price without adding another large panel.

### Alignment

Alignment compares the scaled Sentiment MA 21 path against the price path.

- Sentiment premium: scaled sentiment is materially above the price path
- Mild sentiment premium: scaled sentiment is modestly above the price path
- Aligned: scaled sentiment and price are close
- Mild price premium: price is modestly above scaled sentiment
- Price premium: price is materially above scaled sentiment

The word scaled matters. This is not raw sentiment and it is not valuation.

### Decision Pressure

Decision Pressure appears in the Sentiment MA 21 hover.

It describes unresolved setup tension, including:

- pressure label
- DP score
- skew
- source
- gate

Decision Pressure is context. It is not a standalone trade signal.

## Product language policy

Use language that explains participation, narrative structure, attention, validation, setup quality, and pressure.

Avoid direct price predictions or trade instructions.
