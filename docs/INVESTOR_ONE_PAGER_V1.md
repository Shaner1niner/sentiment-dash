# SETA Investor One-Pager v1

This document is a compact investor/product narrative. It should be adapted for the audience before external use.

## One-line summary

SETA is a behavioral market intelligence platform that explains behavior beneath price by combining market structure, sentiment, attention, source breadth, and timing context into clear research briefings.

## Problem

Market participants have more charts, headlines, social feeds, and indicators than ever, but the interpretation layer is fragmented.

Common gaps:

- price charts show movement but not behavioral context
- sentiment feeds can overstate narrow amplification
- attention spikes are often mistaken for validation
- news and social data are hard to compare across assets
- users need faster ways to separate meaningful setups from noise
- public commentary must avoid overconfident or advisory language

## Solution

SETA organizes behavioral evidence into an explainable dashboard and briefing workflow.

The system combines:

- price and overlap structure
- sentiment state
- attention context
- source/authorship breadth
- indicator confirmation
- event timelines
- Market Tape ranking
- public-safe explanatory language

The product does not tell users what to buy or sell. It helps them understand what the evidence says and what deserves further research.

## Why now

Markets are increasingly shaped by narrative velocity, social participation, fragmented attention, and rapid cross-asset information flow.

Existing charting tools are strong at price visualization. SETA targets the behavioral interpretation layer that sits between raw data and human research judgment.

- Market Tape ranking

The product is already useful as a public proof of concept while the SaaS layer is developed.

## Differentiation

SETA is differentiated by:

- behavioral interpretation rather than charting alone
- public/member mode discipline
- source breadth as a trust qualifier
- explainable, non-advisory briefing language
- combined price/sentiment/attention context
- AI-ready structured briefing schema
- practical migration path from static dashboard to SaaS

## Source breadth trust layer

SETA treats authorship/source breadth as a confidence layer.

This helps distinguish broader participation from narrow amplification by a small number of loud voices or repeated sources. The product does not treat breadth as proof. It presents breadth as a qualifier with channel-specific caveats for X, news, and other sources.

This trust layer can become a durable differentiator for member, pro, and enterprise products.

## Business model

Likely monetization path:

- Free/Public: educational dashboard and limited briefing access
- Member Research: broader asset coverage, richer Market Tape, briefing depth, trust detail
- Pro/Creator: exports, report workflows, saved briefings, publishing support
- Enterprise/Research: API, custom coverage, teams, data lineage, compliance support

Pricing should be validated after the member workflow is clearly understood.

## Roadmap

Near-term roadmap:

1. continue polishing the public/member dashboard
2. strengthen Briefing Mode as the main explanatory surface
3. add AI-assisted briefing generation using a structured schema
4. introduce auth and entitlements
5. add saved views, briefing history, and alerts
6. add billing
7. migrate durable data and jobs into a SaaS architecture

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Data/API limitations | confidence labels, source caveats, and visible trust metadata |
| Regulatory/advice risk | educational framing, public-safe language, no buy/sell calls |
| Overbuilding SaaS too early | phased migration from the current working dashboard |
| AI hallucination or overconfidence | structured inputs, constrained outputs, versioned schemas, human review |
| Signal overinterpretation | breadth and attention framed as context, not proof |
