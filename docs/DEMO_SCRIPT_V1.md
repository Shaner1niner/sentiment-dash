# SETA Demo Script v1

This script is for founder-led demos, investor conversations, early customer discovery, and product walkthroughs. It should stay educational and non-advisory.

## Demo goal

Explain SETA in under five minutes:

SETA helps users understand behavior beneath price by combining price structure, sentiment, attention, source breadth, and timing context into an explainable market dashboard.

The demo should not pitch SETA as a prediction engine, trading signal service, or buy/sell tool.

## Setup

Use the live GitHub Pages dashboard unless a local branch is being reviewed.

Suggested starting view:

- public dashboard
- BTC or another clean high-context asset
- daily or weekly frequency
- Briefing view
- Combined Overlap or Canonical Overlap depending on which view is most legible
- Market Tape visible

If showing member value, switch to the member dashboard after the public story is clear.

## Five-minute flow

### 1. Open with the category

Suggested language:

> SETA is a behavioral market intelligence dashboard. It is designed to explain what may be happening underneath price, not to tell someone what to buy or sell.

Key points:

- price still matters
- sentiment is context
- attention is not validation
- source breadth helps qualify trust

### 2. Show the briefing surface

Point to the Briefing Mode summary first.

Explain:

- the selected asset and timeframe
- current behavioral state
- what is strengthening or weakening
- whether evidence looks broad or narrow
- what the dashboard is watching next

Suggested language:

> The goal is to make the chart interpretable quickly. Instead of asking someone to decode every indicator first, SETA gives a compact research read and then lets them inspect the evidence.

### 3. Explain breadth as a trust check

Show the breadth/trust badge or briefing trust language.

Suggested language:

> Breadth helps us ask whether participation looks broad or whether the move may be coming from a narrower set of louder sources. It is not proof of organic demand, but it is a useful confidence check.

Mention caveats:

- X breadth can be limited by API sampling
- news breadth can be distorted by syndication and repeated coverage
- breadth is a qualifier, not a signal by itself

### 4. Show Market Tape

Use the ranked cards to explain how SETA surfaces candidates.

Suggested language:

> The Market Tape is the triage layer. It ranks assets by current behavioral setup so the user can decide where to spend research time.

Avoid implying that the top card is a trade recommendation.

### 5. Drill into chart evidence

Scroll to price and indicator panes.

Explain only what is visible:

- overlap state
- event markers
- MACD/RSI/Stoch context
- attention and sentiment confirmation or divergence

Suggested language:

> The chart is the evidence layer. The briefing gives the read; the chart shows why the read exists.

### 6. Show public versus member logic

If relevant, switch to member mode.

Explain:

- public mode is selective and education-oriented
- member mode has broader coverage and richer controls
- both modes preserve non-advisory language

### 7. Close with the roadmap

Suggested language:

> The current product is a working public dashboard. The next major layer is AI-assisted briefings, then account features like saved views, billing, and alerts.

## What to avoid

Avoid:

- buy/sell language
- guaranteed outcomes
- price targets
- personalized advice
- claiming breadth proves organic demand
- calling Market Tape a trading signal
- implying SETA replaces human judgment

Better phrasing:

- "this setup is showing..."
- "the current read is..."
- "the evidence is mixed because..."
- "this deserves follow-up research..."
- "breadth improves confidence, but does not prove..."

## Common questions

### Is this a trading tool?

Suggested answer:

SETA is a research and education tool. It helps interpret market behavior, but it is not designed to provide personalized investment advice or buy/sell instructions.

### Why combine sentiment with price?

Suggested answer:

Price shows what happened. Sentiment, attention, and breadth help explain the behavioral context around that move.

### Why does source breadth matter?

Suggested answer:

It helps qualify whether participation appears broad or concentrated. That matters because a sentiment move driven by many independent sources is different from a move dominated by a few loud voices.

### Does SETA predict future price?

Suggested answer:

No. SETA organizes evidence and context. It can highlight pressure, deterioration, repair, and confirmation, but it should not be framed as a prediction engine.

### What is the business model?

Suggested answer:

The likely model is a public education surface, a paid member research tier, and later pro or enterprise offerings for richer briefings, saved views, alerts, exports, and API-style access.

## Demo success criteria

A strong demo leaves the audience understanding:

- what SETA is
- why behavior beneath price matters
- how Briefing Mode summarizes the read
- why breadth improves trust calibration
- why public/member mode separation matters
- what the SaaS roadmap unlocks next
