How SETA reads the market.

SETA organizes market emotion, setup quality, and participation context. It is a research and education layer — not a price target, trade instruction, or buy/sell recommendation.

Structure Score

Structure Score is SETA's 0–100 read on setup quality: how coherent the current sentiment, price, technical, confirmation, and participation structure looks.

Structure Trend

Improving, softening, or stable describes the recent direction of travel in Structure Score, not a guaranteed future move.

Signal State

Signal State combines the current bias and setup stage. Example: Bias: Bearish · Stage: Momentum means pressure is active, not exhausted.

Participation Quality

Participation Quality asks whether the read is broadly supported or distorted by thin, noisy, concentrated activity. It affects confidence, not direction by itself.

Evidence

Evidence is the receipt layer: visible dashboard/chart receipts first, SETA system notes second, and external context only when relevant and verified.

Overlap Band

Overlap Band is high-value context for sentiment/price separation, but it is optional. If it does not materially change the read, SETA can silently omit it.

Research Mode

Research Mode contains the deeper receipts behind the read: source mix, drivers, confirmation context, and additional evidence trails when available.

AI / model badge

The AI/model badge is a context flag, not a standalone signal. It should be read alongside structure, confirmation, and participation quality.

Chart layers

Structure = setup coherence. Momentum = continuation force. Pressure = unresolved tension. Timing = extension or mean-reversion risk.

Default public read format:

Asset: BTC
View: Daily / 3M
Read: Weak structure, softening

SETA explains market emotion and setup quality. It does not tell readers what to buy or sell.
